\paragraph{Scope of the matrix iteration gain.}
At the frozen parent-selected step sizes, QuotientOpt reaches the target in 2/3 initializations and AdamW in 3/3; QuotientOpt is faster in 1/3 paired checks. The seed-42 iteration gain therefore does not establish an initialization-uniform advantage. All outcomes, including unreached targets, remain in Appendix~\ref{app:initialization-confirmation}; the local-basin theory does not assert uniform global capture from these initializations.
