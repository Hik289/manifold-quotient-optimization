\section{Conclusion}
Balanced quotient geometry gives an explicit product metric and a
coordinate-consistent first-moment update. Second-order pullback
cancellation, residual-gap spectra, Schur analysis, and Hessian
whitening yield sharp local curvature, momentum, and statistical
constants under the stated assumptions. Completed controls connect
horizontal memory to gauge consistency. Graph prediction and SDF
fitting improve validation-selected held-out loss relative to AdamW
under the reported protocols. Full baseline comparisons and
initialization records identify the contribution of balancing and
projection separately. Complete derivations, optimizer settings,
loss histories, and run records accompany these results.
